Description

Stack Order is a 2D narrative roguelite deckbuilder with a strong narrative dimension, driven by hand-drawn art inspired by comic books.

Its core mechanic replaces the classic card play with a stacking system, allowing vertical and horizontal synergies to be created between stacks.

The game revolves around intense duels against opponents with unique abilities and personalities. Each turn, the opponent announces their intentions, turning the game into a tactical puzzle: the player must respond to an immediate threat while building a long-term strategy.

About Us

We are a young studio founded in 2026 by two former French art and design students who are passionate about delivering unique and artistic experiences.

Key Features

  • Unique stacking mechanic: A new approach to card games in which sequences and anticipation create deep strategic layers.
  • Narrative: A deep story of corruption and manipulation told through rich environments and character interactions.
  • Roguelite progression: Refine your deck, boss knowledge, and strategy with each playthrough.
  • Over 100 different cards: Collected from opponents or traded with characters, each card can transform your deck.
