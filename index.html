Our Current Project

A narrative roguelite deckbuilder with comic-book style visuals.

Master the Stacks & Change the Storyline.

Strategic Deckbuilding

Stack Order isn't just about playing cards, it's about layering them. Manipulate the order of operations and craft synergies inside your deck to create devastating combos. Every turn is a puzzle against bosses attacks where your positioning matters as much as your power.

Synergy is key. Combine strong effects and anticipate the outcomes to break through enemy defenses.

A Living Comic Book Unveils

A Colorful Narrative Story

Dive into a 2D comic-inspired world where the story unfolds panel by panel. Between high-stakes boss battles, navigate through a deep and original setting.

Throughout your journey, you will encounter various friendly faces that can alter the deck you bring into battle.

Clever Boss Battles

In Stack Order, each fight is designed to make you feel its intensity. You'll only face off against uniquely designed bosses, each with their own set of mechanics and personality.

Learn their patterns, adapt your deck, and strike when their guard is down.
